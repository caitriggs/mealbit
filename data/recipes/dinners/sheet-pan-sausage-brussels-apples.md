**Why this week:** November is when Brussels sprouts come to the market on the stalk and
storage apples are at their crispest, and the two of them roasted on one pan with sausage
is the whole dinner, no sides.

**The move:** **roast in two stages.** The sprouts and onion need a head start on a hot
pan so their cut faces go dark and sweet before anything else lands; the apples and
sausages go on after, so the apples hold their shape instead of collapsing. The glaze is
maple syrup, whole-grain mustard, apple cider vinegar and a grated garlic clove, and it
goes on **twice**: half before the second roast, where it caramelises onto the sausage
casings, and half over the hot pan at the end so it still tastes sharp.

1. Oven to 425°F with a sheet pan inside. Halve the sprouts (quarter the big ones), cut
   the red onion into eighths through the root, and toss both with 2 tbsp olive oil and a
   big pinch of kosher salt on the hot pan. Cut side down where you can. Roast 12 minutes.
2. Glaze while they roast: 3 tbsp maple syrup, 2 tbsp whole-grain mustard, 1 tbsp apple
   cider vinegar and 1 garlic clove grated in. Whisk.
3. Core the apples and cut them into thick wedges — eight to an apple, no thinner, or they
   turn to sauce. Leave the sausages whole; pricked sausages lose their juice.
4. Pull the pan, push the vegetables to the edges, lay the sausages in the middle and the
   apples around them. Spoon over half the glaze and turn everything once. Back in for
   18 minutes, until the sausages read 160°F and the sprout leaves on the edge are
   crisp and dark.
5. Rest of the glaze over the whole hot pan straight from the oven. A lot of black pepper,
   a handful of torn parsley. Slice the sausages thick at the table. Half the loaf,
   torn, for the glaze on the pan.

**Leftover plan:** slice the leftover sausages into coins and pack them with the sprouts,
apples and onion, scraping the pan glaze over the top. It eats well at room temperature
or after a minute in the microwave, and a wedge of the bread goes in the box as it is.
