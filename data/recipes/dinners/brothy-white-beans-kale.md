**Why this week:** the cheapest dinner in the library and the one most likely to become a
default. It is also almost entirely from the cupboard, which makes it the recipe to keep
when the market run goes sideways.

**The move:** **the Parmigiano rind goes in the pot.** Save them in the freezer; one rind
simmered in the broth for 20 minutes adds a savory backbone that nothing else does. And
**smash a third of the beans** against the side of the pot — that's what turns thin bean
soup into something with body, without cream or flour.

1. Onion and diced fennel in a generous amount of olive oil, 10 minutes, soft and sweet.
2. 4 sliced garlic cloves, 3 anchovy fillets, 1 tsp chili flakes — cook until the
   anchovies dissolve completely, 2 minutes. (Leave them out for vegetarian; add an extra
   pinch of salt and a splash of soy.)
3. Beans with their liquid, 3 cups stock, rosemary sprig, bay, the Parmigiano rind.
   Simmer 20 minutes.
4. Smash about a third of the beans against the pot wall with a wooden spoon. Stir.
   It'll thicken visibly.
5. Kale, stemmed and torn, in for the last 6 minutes.
6. Toast: thick sourdough slices, grilled or broiled, rubbed hard with a raw garlic clove
   while still hot, then flooded with olive oil and salt.
7. Bowls: beans, a lot of olive oil, lemon zest, chili flakes, grated Parmigiano, toast.

**Leftover plan:** thickens overnight into something closer to a stew — loosen with a
splash of water. Pack with a wedge of lemon and a small jar of chili oil to finish at the
desk.
