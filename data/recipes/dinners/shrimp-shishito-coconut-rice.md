**Why this week:** shishitos have a short local window and about one in ten is
volcanically hot, which turns dinner into a game. That's the entire appeal.

**The move:** coconut rice cooked with a **1:1 coconut milk to water** ratio, then left covered and *off the heat* for a full 10 minutes. Rice cooked in
pure coconut milk scorches on the bottom every time; half water is the fix nobody
mentions.

1. Rice: 2 cups jasmine, rinsed, with 1 can coconut milk + 1½ cups water + 1 tsp salt.
   Boil, lid on, lowest heat 12 minutes, then off the heat 10 minutes. Don't peek.
2. Dry the shrimp on a towel — properly dry. Toss with salt.
3. Ripping-hot pan, thin film of oil. Shishitos in a single layer, undisturbed, until
   blistered and blackening, 4–5 minutes. Out, hit with flaky salt and lime.
4. Same pan hotter still. Shrimp in one layer, 90 seconds a side, no more. Add grated
   garlic and ginger for the last 30 seconds.
5. Off heat: 2 tbsp fish sauce, 1 tbsp honey, juice of 2 limes, tossed through.
6. Shrimp and peppers over the coconut rice, scallions and torn Thai basil over
   everything.

**Leftover plan:** shrimp go rubbery reheated, so don't. Pack them cold over the coconut
rice as a rice salad with extra lime and herbs — it's a better lunch than it was a dinner.
