**Why this week:** April has asparagus and not much else, and charring it hard before it
goes into an enchilada keeps it from turning to string in the oven.

**The move:** **fry the tortillas in oil for 15 seconds a side before rolling.** This is
the step every home recipe skips and every taquería does. It waterproofs the tortilla so
it can sit in sauce for 25 minutes without dissolving into paste. Fifteen seconds, a
paper towel, done. Second move: **poach the chicken in the salsa verde itself**, then
shred it back into that liquid — you get seasoned meat and a thinned, chicken-flavored
sauce out of one pot.

1. Chicken thighs into a pan with 2 cups salsa verde, ½ cup stock, 1 tsp cumin, 1 tsp
   oregano, half a sliced white onion. Simmer covered 20 minutes, until shreddable.
2. Chicken out, shredded with two forks. A cup of the cooking liquid stirred back into
   the meat; the rest is your sauce — loosen it with stock to a pourable consistency.
3. Asparagus, snapped and cut into 1-inch lengths, into a dry ripping-hot pan until
   **blackened in spots**, 4 minutes. Salt. Half goes into the filling, half stays out
   for the top.
4. Filling: shredded chicken, half the asparagus, half the grated Jack, lime juice, salt.
5. **Fry the tortillas** — ¼ inch of oil, medium-high, 15 seconds a side, until pliable
   and just starting to blister. Drain on paper.
6. Sauce a thin layer into a baking dish. Roll each tortilla around filling, seam down,
   packed tight in the dish. Remaining sauce poured over — **cover the edges or they burn**
   — then the rest of the cheese.
7. 400°F, 20–25 minutes, until bubbling and browned in patches.
8. Crema, crumbled queso fresco, the reserved asparagus, cilantro, spring onions, lime.

**Per plate:** the salsa verde is blended without cilantro; it is strewn over the finished pan, so serve the no-cilantro plate from one end and scatter the other.

**Leftover plan:** enchiladas are one of the few baked dishes that are genuinely better
reheated — the sauce has had a night to soak in. Cut squares, pack with a wedge of lime
and extra crema.
