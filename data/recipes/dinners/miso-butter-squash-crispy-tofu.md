**Why this week:** delicata is the one winter squash you don't have to peel — the skin
roasts to something like a potato chip. It shows up in September and it is gone by
Christmas.

**The move:** **press the tofu, then dredge it in cornstarch.** Twenty minutes under
something heavy pulls out enough water that the surface can actually dehydrate; the
cornstarch does the rest. Untreated tofu roasted at 425°F is a sponge, and no amount of
sauce fixes it.

1. Tofu: drain, cut into 1-inch cubes, press between towels under a heavy pan, 20
   minutes. Toss with 2 tbsp neutral oil, 3 tbsp cornstarch, 1 tsp salt.
2. Oven 425°F, two sheet pans, both preheating empty. A hot pan is half the crisp.
3. Squash halved, seeded, cut into ½-inch half-moons — skin on, don't peel it.
   Tossed with oil and salt. Onto one hot pan.
4. Tofu onto the other hot pan, well spaced. Both in, 25–30 minutes, flipping once.
5. Miso butter: mash 3 tbsp softened butter with 2 tbsp white miso, 1 tbsp maple syrup,
   1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 grated garlic clove.
6. Bok choy, halved, seared cut-side down in a hot pan, 3 minutes, then a splash of water
   and a lid for 2 more.
7. Toss the hot squash with the miso butter straight out of the oven so it melts into
   every surface. Tofu, greens, rice. Chili crunch, scallions, sesame over everything.

**Leftover plan:** everything here is good cold or at room temperature, which is rare.
Build the lunch boxes directly off the sheet pans while they're still warm.
