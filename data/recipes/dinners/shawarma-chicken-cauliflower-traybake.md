**Why this week:** the lowest-effort dinner here — fifteen minutes of hands-on work — and
the one that generates the best leftovers by a distance. Good for a week with a
late meeting.

**The move:** the spice blend is **cinnamon-forward**, which is what separates shawarma
from generic "Middle Eastern seasoning." A quarter teaspoon of cinnamon in a savory rub
reads as depth, not dessert. And roast the cauliflower **cut-side down** and undisturbed:
flat contact with hot metal is how you get a browned face instead of pale florets.

1. Rub: 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp coriander, 1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp turmeric, ½ tsp
   cinnamon, 1 tsp black pepper, 4 grated garlic cloves, 3 tbsp olive oil, big pinch
   salt. Half onto the chicken, half onto the cauliflower.
2. Oven 425°F. Cauliflower broken into large flat-faced florets, cut-side down on the
   pan. Red onion in thick wedges tucked around. Chicken on a second pan.
3. Both in, 30–35 minutes. Don't stir the cauliflower.
4. Tahini sauce: ⅓ cup tahini, juice of a lemon, 1 grated garlic clove, ½ tsp salt, then
   ice water whisked in a tablespoon at a time. **It will seize and look ruined — keep
   going.** Three more tablespoons of water and it turns pale and pourable. This is the
   single most useful sauce in this whole library.
5. Slice the chicken. Pita, everything piled on, tahini over, parsley and a squeeze of
   lemon.

**Leftover plan:** the reason this recipe exists. Chicken + cauliflower + tahini over
grains is a genuinely excellent lunch bowl, and the tahini sauce keeps a week in a jar.
Make double sauce.
