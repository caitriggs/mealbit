**Why this week:** the cheapest good dinner in the library, and the one that most reliably
makes people say "wait, there's no meat in this."

**The move:** two things, and neither is the filling. **(1) Quick-pickle the onion first**
— it needs 20 minutes and it's what makes the taco taste like a taquería instead of a
casserole. **(2) Char the tortillas directly on the burner flame**, 15 seconds a side,
until they're spotted black. A warm tortilla is fine; a charred one is the meal.

1. Pickle: thin-slice a red onion, cover with ½ cup cider vinegar, ½ cup hot water,
   1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp salt. Leave it. It'll go shocking pink.
2. Oven 450°F. Sweet potatoes in ¾-inch cubes, tossed with oil, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp
   smoked paprika, 1 tsp coriander, salt. Single layer, 25–30 minutes, flipped once,
   until the edges are genuinely dark.
3. Beans: drained, into a pan with olive oil, sliced garlic, 1 minced chipotle plus a
   spoon of its adobo, 1 tsp oregano and a splash of water. Smash about a third of them
   with the back of a spoon — that's what makes them cling to a tortilla.
4. Cabbage shredded thin, tossed with lime juice and salt, squeezed a little with your
   hands.
5. Char the tortillas over the flame.
6. Build: beans, sweet potato, cabbage, pickled onion, crema, cotija, cilantro, avocado.

**Per plate:** tacos are built one at a time — the no-cilantro plate gets everything but the cilantro.

**Leftover plan:** don't pack assembled tacos — they'll be sad. Pack the components as a
bowl over rice or the leftover cabbage slaw, tortillas separate. The pickled onions get
better all week and are worth making double.
