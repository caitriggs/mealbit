**Why this week:** February has nothing green to offer, so this leans on leeks, potatoes
and a jar — and comes out looking like a restaurant plate anyway.

**The move:** **bloom the saffron in warm liquid for 10 minutes before it goes near the
pot.** Saffron dropped dry into a simmering broth gives you maybe a third of its color and
flavor. Crush the threads between your fingers into 3 tbsp of warm stock and leave them.
Second move: **the fish is poached off the heat.** Bring the broth up, slide the fillets
in, lid on, burner OFF. Eight minutes. It will not overcook, which is the failure mode of
every home cod dish.

1. Bloom the saffron in warm stock.
2. Leeks sliced and washed hard (grit hides in the layers), softened in olive oil 8
   minutes without color.
3. 2 tsp smoked paprika, 30 seconds. ½ cup white wine, cooked off.
4. Potatoes in ½-inch coins, 1 can of tomatoes crushed by hand, 3 cups stock, 2 bay leaves, the
   saffron and its liquid, sliced roasted peppers. Simmer 15–18 minutes until the
   potatoes are just tender.
5. Season the broth aggressively — it should taste slightly *too* salty on its own,
   because the fish is unseasoned.
6. Fish salted, laid on top, lid on, **heat off**, 8 minutes.
7. A teaspoon of sherry vinegar over the top at the end, parsley, olive oil, bread.

**Leftover plan:** pack the broth and potatoes with the fish flaked in; it reheats gently
in a mug or a pan. The bread does not survive — buy a fresh half loaf or skip it.
