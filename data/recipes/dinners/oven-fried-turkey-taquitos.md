**Why this week:** taquitos are a genuinely fun dinner, and the oven does the frying. The
version worth making swaps the jar of salsa for charred tomatillos, which takes eight
minutes and is not remotely the same thing.

**The move:** **broil the tomatillos and jalapeños until they're black and collapsing,
then blend them with raw onion.** Raw tomatillo salsa is sharp and thin;
charred tomatillo salsa is smoky, sweet and thick enough to sit on a taquito instead of
running off it. Second move: **brush the taquitos with oil and bake them seam-side down**
— seam-side down is what stops them unrolling, and no toothpicks are needed.

1. Oven to 425°F, and the broiler on first. Tomatillos husked, rinsed of their stickiness,
   halved. Onto a foiled tray with the jalapeños and half the onion in wedges. **Broil
   8–10 minutes until blackened in patches and slumping.**
2. Blitz the charred lot with the juice of a lime, 1 tsp sugar
   and plenty of salt. Leave it chunky. Taste it — it should be bright and a little smoky.
3. **Make your own taco seasoning** (it's just this): 2 tsp cumin, 2 tsp coriander,
   1 tsp smoked paprika, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp chili flakes, 1 tsp salt.
4. Turkey browned hard in oil, pressed flat and left 3 minutes before breaking up —
   ground turkey is lean and turns to grey mush if you fuss it. Grated garlic and the
   spice mix, 1 minute. A splash of water and ¼ cup of the salsa, cooked until it's
   thick and clinging, not wet. **Wet filling makes soggy taquitos.**
5. Warm the tortillas so they roll without cracking — 20 seconds in a dry pan each, or
   the whole stack wrapped in a damp towel in the microwave for 45 seconds.
6. Roll: 3 tbsp filling and a pinch of cheese in a line on one half, rolled tight, laid
   **seam-side down** on an oiled sheet pan. Brush the tops and sides generously with oil.
7. Bake 15–20 minutes, rotating once, until deep gold and blistered.
8. Salsa, crema drizzled from a spoon, finely diced red onion, cilantro, pickled jalapeños.

**Per plate:** the tomatillo salsa is now blended WITHOUT cilantro — charred tomatillo, jalapeño, raw onion and lime carry it on their own. Cilantro goes on the assembled taquitos at the end, over the plates that want it.

**Leftover plan:** taquitos re-crisp perfectly — 6 minutes in a hot oven or a toaster
oven and they're as good as new. Pack the salsa and crema in separate small jars.
