**Why it's on the list:** a cup of full-fat cottage cheese is about 25g of protein, it
needs no cooking and no reheating, and the sweet version is the one that actually gets
eaten. The savoury version — chili crisp, smashed cucumber, everything seasoning — was
tried and rejected: *"chilli oil and cucumber sounds gross."*

**The move:** **buy 4% full-fat, not low-fat.** Low-fat cottage cheese is sour, rubbery
and watery, and it is single-handedly responsible for most people believing they dislike
cottage cheese. Full-fat is mild and creamy and tastes of fresh milk. Second move:
**toast the almonds.** Thirty seconds in a dry pan, done once for the whole week, is the
difference between a crunchy bowl and a soft one.

1. Toast the sliced almonds until they smell nutty. Cool them completely before they go
   in a jar — warm nuts steam themselves soft. Once for the week.
2. A cup of cottage cheese into the container. A tablespoon of maple over it, a drop of vanilla, a
   pinch of flaky salt.
3. Berries on top, **whole, not cut** — cut fruit weeps, and by lunchtime you have pink
   liquid in the bottom of the tub.
4. Almonds in a separate twist of foil, added at the desk so they stay loud.
5. A slice of seeded toast alongside, or crackers.

**At the desk:** stir it once before you start. The maple sits on top otherwise, so the
first three bites are sweet and the rest are plain.

**Note:** nut butter and a spoonful of preserve is the other version of this and works
identically — swap the maple, berries and almonds for 2 tbsp of almond or peanut butter
and a good jam. Same container, same five minutes.
