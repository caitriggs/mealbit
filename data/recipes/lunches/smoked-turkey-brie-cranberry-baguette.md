**Why it's on the list:** November's sandwich. Smoked turkey, soft cheese and the sharp
sweetness of cranberry is the plate everyone wants the Friday after Thanksgiving, and a
baguette makes it a lunch you can hold in one hand on a cold day.

**The move:** **cranberry sauce on the bread, not on the turkey.** Spread on the bottom
half it soaks into the crumb and behaves like a jam; spooned over the meat it slides out
of the back of the sandwich with the first bite. Second move: **slice the apple paper-thin
on a mandoline** — thick apple makes the sandwich hinge open; thin apple layers flat and
gives crunch without fighting the bread.

1. Half a baguette split lengthwise. 2 tsp Dijon on the top half, ¼ cup whole-berry
   cranberry sauce on the bottom, spread to the ends.
2. 4 oz Brie in thin slices over the cranberry. The turkey in loose folds on top of it.
3. Apple sliced paper-thin, a few leaves of butter lettuce, black pepper. Close, press,
   cut into two.

**Note:** it holds four hours wrapped in parchment, which is the whole point of a
baguette sandwich. Keep the apple for the morning if it has to be made the night before.
