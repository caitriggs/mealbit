**Why it's on the list:** the emergency lunch. Every ingredient lives in the cupboard
permanently, so this is the answer to "there's nothing in the house" that isn't
DoorDash.

**The move:** **rub the hot toast with a raw garlic clove.** The toast acts as a grater;
the clove melts into the surface. It takes four seconds and it is the entire difference
between beans on toast and something you'd order.

1. Thick sourdough, toasted hard — properly dark, or it collapses under the beans.
2. **Rubbed with a halved raw garlic clove while still hot**, then flooded with olive
   oil and salt.
3. Beans drained, smashed roughly with a fork with 2 tbsp olive oil, lemon juice and
   zest, 1 tsp red wine vinegar, a pinch of chili flakes, salt, pepper. Leave it lumpy.
4. Piled on. Chili crisp, shaved Parmigiano, parsley, more olive oil than seems
   reasonable. Tomato if it's August.

**Per plate:** the smash is lemon, olive oil and chili — complete on its own. August tomato goes on the toast that wants it only.
