**Why it works all week:** it is a filling, not a finished dish, which is exactly why it
doesn't get boring — sandwich Monday, lettuce cups Wednesday, on top of the leftover rice
Friday.

**The move:** **shred the chicken while it's still warm and salt it immediately**, before
any dressing goes near it. Warm meat takes seasoning; cold dressed meat just wears it.
And use **half mayo, half yogurt** — full mayo goes heavy by Wednesday.

1. Chicken pulled off the bird in shreds, not cubes. Salted while warm.
2. Dressing: ½ cup mayo, ½ cup Greek yogurt, the can of green chiles, 2 tbsp chopped
   pickled jalapeños plus 1 tbsp of their brine, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1 tsp
   smoked paprika, 2 grated garlic cloves, juice of a lime, lots of black pepper.
3. Folded together with sliced celery and scallions. Taste for salt and lime — it should
   be brighter than feels right, because it flattens in the fridge.
4. Five containers. **Cilantro, pepitas and avocado stay out.**

**At the desk:** pile on bread or into a tortilla, then cilantro, toasted pepitas, avocado,
lime. Toasting the bread is worth the ninety seconds.

**Per plate:** already built this way: the cilantro, pepitas and avocado are packed separately and go on at the desk, so the no-cilantro container simply never gets the cilantro jar.
