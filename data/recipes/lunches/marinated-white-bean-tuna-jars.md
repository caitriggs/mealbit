**Why it works all week:** fifteen minutes, no stove, and it is the only lunch here that
is *unambiguously better on Friday than on Monday* — the beans keep drinking the dressing.

**The move:** **buy the tuna packed in olive oil, not water, and don't drain it — that oil
is a third of the dressing.** Water-packed tuna is a different, sadder ingredient and no
amount of mayonnaise fixes it.

1. Dressing: ⅓ cup olive oil, 3 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tbsp Dijon, 2 grated garlic
   cloves, 2 tbsp capers, 1 tsp oregano, ½ tsp chili flakes, plenty of salt and pepper.
2. Beans drained and rinsed, tossed in the dressing with thin-sliced red onion, celery,
   halved olives, chopped sun-dried tomatoes. **Sit 20 minutes** before packing.
3. Tuna flaked in with its oil. Lemon zest and juice.
4. Five jars. Parsley goes in **at the desk**, not in the jar — it blackens.

**At the desk:** parsley, a crack of pepper, a lemon wedge. Good on its own, better on
toast, best with a hard-boiled egg if there's one going.
