**Why it's on the list:** the deli sandwich that actually tastes like something: cold rare
beef, a cheddar sharp enough to notice, and a slaw that does the job of both the pickle
and the mustard without a pickle in sight.

**The move:** **the slaw is the condiment.** Cabbage shaved thin and apple grated or
sliced thin, dressed with cider vinegar and a horseradish mayo, gives you heat, acid,
crunch and sweetness in one layer. Pile it high; it collapses as you press the sandwich.
Second move: **buy the beef rare and sliced thin** — a thick, grey slice is a different
sandwich.

1. Slaw: a quarter head of cabbage shaved thin, half an apple cut into matchsticks.
   Toss with 1 tbsp cider vinegar and a pinch of salt.
2. Dressing: 2 tbsp mayonnaise, 1 tbsp prepared horseradish, 1 tsp Dijon. Half of it
   into the slaw.
3. The rest of the dressing on four slices of rye. 3 oz cheddar in slices, the roast beef
   in loose folds, a big handful of slaw, black pepper. Top slice, press, cut in two.

**Note:** the slaw keeps two days dressed and gets better; make it Sunday and the
sandwich is a two-minute build.
