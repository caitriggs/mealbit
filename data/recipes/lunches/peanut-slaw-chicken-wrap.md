**Why it's on the list:** the designated home for whatever chicken is left over, and the
crunchiest thing on this list.

**The move:** **keep the peanuts whole until the last second and crush them by hand into
the wrap.** Pre-chopped peanuts are dust by lunchtime. And make the peanut dressing
**thicker than a dressing** — closer to a paste — so it doesn't run out the end of the
wrap at your desk.

1. Dressing: 3 tbsp peanut butter, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tsp sesame oil,
   1 tsp honey, 1 tsp grated ginger, 1 grated garlic clove, sriracha to taste. Loosen
   with **only** a teaspoon or two of water.
2. Cabbage and carrot shredded thin, tossed with the dressing and the juice of a lime.
3. Chicken folded through.
4. Tortilla warmed 20 seconds. Filling in a line, **peanuts crushed over by hand**,
   cilantro, scallions.
5. Rolled tight, wrapped in foil, cut on the diagonal.

**Note:** the slaw can be dressed the night before — cabbage is one of the few things
that improves.

**Per plate:** the slaw and its peanut dressing carry the wrap. Cilantro is laid in the line as you roll, so roll the no-cilantro one without it.
