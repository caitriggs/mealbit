**Why it's on the list:** a five-minute lunch that beats the $16 version down the street.
This is the whole thesis of the newsletter compressed into one sandwich.

**The move:** **cheese on both slices, tuna in the middle, and butter the outsides — cook
it like a grilled cheese in a pan, not open-faced under a broiler.** The broiler version
is a bready open sandwich with hot tuna on it. The pan version is a sealed, crisp, molten
thing. Also: **do not drain the oil-packed tuna.** It's the dressing.

1. Mix (30 seconds): 1 tin tuna with its oil, 1 tbsp mayo, 1 tsp Dijon, 1 tsp capers,
   1 tbsp finely diced red onion, black pepper.
2. Two slices of bread, buttered on the outside. Cheddar on the inside face of both.
3. Tuna and a few pickled jalapeño rings in the middle.
4. Medium-low pan, lid on, **3 minutes a side.** Low and covered is what melts the cheese
   before the bread burns.

**Note:** the tuna mix can be made Sunday and kept in a jar, which drops this to two
minutes.
