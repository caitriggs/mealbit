**Why it's on the list:** vegetarian, portable, holds for hours in a bag without going
soggy, and costs about $2.50.

**The move:** **grate the carrots on the big holes and dress them at least a few minutes
ahead** — a dry carrot wrap is sawdust; a dressed one is a slaw. And **warm the tortilla
for 20 seconds before rolling.** A cold tortilla cracks along the fold and the whole
thing leaks by 12:30.

1. Carrots grated, tossed with 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp honey,
   ½ tsp cumin, salt.
2. Hummus mixed with 1 tbsp harissa and a squeeze of lemon.
3. Tortilla warmed 20 seconds. Hummus spread to the edges — **it's the glue**, so don't
   be shy.
4. Carrot slaw in a line off-center, feta crumbled, pine nuts, parsley, za'atar.
5. Rolled tight, **wrapped in foil**, cut on the diagonal. The foil is what holds it
   together in a bag.
