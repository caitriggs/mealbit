**Why it's on the list:** the $15 deli sub costs about $4 to build and takes four minutes,
and the jar of giardiniera is doing 80% of the work.

**The move:** **dress the lettuce, not the sandwich.** Toss the shredded romaine in
vinegar, oil, oregano, salt and pepper *first*, then pack it in. It's the trick every good
Italian deli uses — the seasoning lands on every bite instead of soaking one side of the
bread.

1. Shred the romaine thin. Toss with 1 tbsp red wine vinegar, 1 tbsp olive oil, ½ tsp
   oregano, salt, pepper.
2. Roll split, **a thin layer of mayo on the top half and Dijon on the bottom** — mayo
   waterproofs the bread against the tomato.
3. Provolone, then turkey folded into ribbons rather than laid flat (loft, not slabs).
4. Giardiniera, chopped roughly, drained a little. Tomato. The dressed lettuce last.
5. Wrapped tight in parchment and left five minutes if you can — it compresses and
   improves.

**Per plate:** the giardiniera and the mayo-dressed lettuce are what make the sandwich; the tomato is a layer. Build the no-tomato sub without it and give the other a double.
