**Why it's on the list:** the sandwich every good Italian deli is selling right now and
none of them for under $16. Mortadella is soft, mild and faintly spiced; burrata makes it
rich; pistachios and lemon make it interesting. No pickles, no vinegar, nothing sharp —
the lemon and honey do that work instead.

**The move:** **ruffle the mortadella, don't lay it flat.** A flat stack is a slab you
bite through; loose folds trap air, so every bite is soft and the sandwich stands two
inches tall like the deli version. Second move: **drain the burrata and tear it, don't
slice it** — sliced burrata leaks its cream into the bread; torn and drained, the cream
stays where you put it.

1. Pistachios roughly chopped. Focaccia split horizontally, cut sides up.
2. 2 tbsp pesto loosened with 1 tsp olive oil and a squeeze of lemon, spread on the
   bottom half to the edges.
3. Mortadella in ruffles, piled high.
4. Burrata drained on a paper towel for a minute, then torn over the mortadella.
5. Pistachios, a thread of honey, lemon zest, flaky salt, black pepper. Top on, pressed
   gently so it holds.

**Note:** if it has to travel, pack the burrata separately in its container and tear it
on at the desk. Everything else holds for hours.
