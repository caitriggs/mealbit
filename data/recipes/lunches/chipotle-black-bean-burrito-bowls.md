**Why it works all week:** the only prepped lunch here that reheats genuinely well, so
it's the one to build when the week looks cold and miserable.
**The move:** **cook the rice, then fold lime juice, zest and a slug of olive oil through
it while it's still hot.** Hot rice absorbs; cold rice repels. It's the difference between
lime rice and rice with lime on it. Second move: **smash a third of the beans** so the
bowl holds together instead of rolling around.
1. Rice cooked, then folded while hot with the juice and zest of 2 limes, 3 tbsp olive
   oil and plenty of salt. **The cilantro stays out of the rice** — it goes in the
   toppings jar.
2. Beans: a yellow onion in oil 8 minutes, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tsp cumin, 1 tsp coriander, 1 tsp oregano,
   1 tsp smoked paprika, then 2 minced chipotles plus a spoon of adobo. Beans with their
   liquid, simmered 12 minutes. **A third of them smashed against the pot.**
3. Quick-pickled red onion (¼ cup cider vinegar, ¼ cup hot water, 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp
   salt — 20 minutes).
4. Five containers: rice, beans, cheese, pickled onion.
5. **Separate jar:** salsa verde, crema, pickled jalapeños. **Avocado and lettuce stay
   home** — cut them the morning you eat them or they're a tragedy.
**At the desk:** microwave the base 90 seconds, then salsa, crema, jalapeños, lettuce,
half an avocado, lime.
**Per plate:** the lime rice is finished with lime, oil and salt only. Cilantro travels in the toppings jar with the pickled onion, so the no-cilantro bowl simply skips it.
