**Why it works all week:** everything here is eaten cold by design, so nothing degrades
waiting for a microwave that's always occupied at 12:30.

**The move:** **glaze the tofu twice.** Roast it plain and cornstarch-dusted for 20
minutes, brush with miso glaze, roast 5 more, brush again out of the oven. A single glaze
before roasting just burns; two thin later coats build an actual lacquer.

1. Tofu pressed 20 minutes, cubed, tossed with 2 tbsp neutral oil and 3 tbsp cornstarch.
   425°F, 20 minutes on a preheated pan.
2. Glaze: 3 tbsp white miso, 2 tbsp maple syrup, 2 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tbsp soy, 1 tbsp
   grated ginger, 1 tsp sesame oil. Brushed on, 5 more minutes, brushed again after.
3. Soba cooked (they take 4 minutes — set a timer, mushy soba is unsalvageable), rinsed
   **cold and hard** to strip the surface starch, tossed with 1 tsp sesame oil.
4. Snap peas blanched 90 seconds, shocked in ice water. Edamame thawed.
5. Dressing: ¼ cup soy, 3 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 tbsp sesame oil, 1 tbsp maple, 1 grated
   garlic clove, 1 tbsp grated ginger.
6. Five containers: soba, peas, edamame, sliced radish, tofu on top. Dressing **in a
   separate jar** — soba drinks it and goes claggy overnight.

**At the desk:** dressing over, sesame seeds, scallions, a squeeze of lemon.
