# Pantry — the four things we actually always have

> last_updated: 2026-09-02

The household: *"you can assume we'll always have salt, black pepper, olive oil, and white vinegar
ONLY."*

Everything else a recipe leans on — soy sauce, cornstarch, cumin, garlic, stock, a jar of
harissa — is **not** assumed. It goes on the shopping list under **Check the cupboard**,
and the shopper decides at shop time whether any of it actually needs buying.

This file used to hold about seventy items, on the theory that not re-buying salt every
week was worth the risk of a missing jar. It wasn't: the theory only holds for things you
genuinely never run out of, and a recipe that quietly assumed hoisin, toasted sesame oil
and rice vinegar was a recipe that could not be cooked on the night.

Matching is on the normalized ingredient name (lowercased, plural-stripped), so
`olive oil` here suppresses `2 tbsp olive oil` in a recipe.

## Always in the house

- salt
- kosher salt
- flaky salt
- sea salt
- black pepper
- olive oil
- extra-virgin olive oil
- white vinegar
- distilled white vinegar

> Note the ones that look close but are NOT here: **red wine vinegar, rice vinegar, apple
> cider vinegar and sherry vinegar** are all their own bottle, and **neutral oil** is not
> olive oil. Each of those earns a line on the check list when a recipe wants it.
