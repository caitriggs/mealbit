# Sourcing at the West Seattle market

> last_updated: 2026-08-30 · verified against seattlefarmersmarkets.org
> Sundays 10am–2pm, year-round, California Ave SW & SW Alaska.

**They publish what's in season.** Neighborhood Farmers Markets runs a weekly newsletter
called **RIPE+READY** — subscribe at seattlefarmersmarkets.org and you get the week's
harvest before you go. That is the single biggest time-saver available here, and it beats
anything this repo can model.

The newsletter prints one short sourcing line a week, drawn from the list below and matched
to what the plan actually needs. Keep it to one line — a wall of vendor notes is the thing
nobody reads twice.

```yaml
# Only what is actually verified. Add stalls after a market run; do not guess.
market:
  name: West Seattle Farmers Market
  when: Sundays 10am–2pm, year-round
  where: California Ave SW between SW Alaska and SW Oregon
  vendors_total: "35+ Washington farms and food producers"
  whats_in_season: "RIPE+READY weekly newsletter — seattlefarmersmarkets.org"

tips:
  - when: any
    text: "Vendors rotate week to week and the market says so itself — if a stall is out, the || fallback on each line is the plan."
  - when: any
    text: "Ask a grower what got picked Friday. Anything cut two days ago is what you want, and they will tell you."
  - when: summer
    text: "Tomatoes and peppers are worth walking the whole row for — prices and ripeness vary a lot stall to stall in peak season."
  - when: early-fall
    text: "Chanterelles show up with the foragers first; go early, they sell out well before 2pm."
  - when: late-fall
    text: "Brussels sprouts on the stalk keep far longer than loose ones and usually cost less."
  - when: deep-winter
    text: "Winter is storage crops and hardy greens — the overwintered stuff is sweeter after a frost, which is a real thing, not folklore."
  - when: early-spring
    text: "Asparagus and purple sprouting broccoli have short windows. When you see them, buy more than the list says."
  - when: late-spring
    text: "Strawberries in late May are the best of the year and freeze well — buy a flat, not a pint."
```
