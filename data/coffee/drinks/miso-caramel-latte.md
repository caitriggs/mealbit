The strangest thing in this library and probably the best. White miso is fermented,
salty and faintly sweet — it does to caramel what salt does, but with about four times
more going on. It reads as "what *is* that" rather than "this has miso in it."

**Caramel (makes ~10):** ¾ cup sugar melted in a dry pan over medium until it's the color
of an old penny — **don't stir, swirl.** Off the heat, whisk in ½ cup warm cream (it will
erupt; that's normal), then 2 tbsp butter, **1½ tbsp white miso**, and 1 tsp vanilla.
Whisk until completely smooth.

**Build:** 1½ tbsp caramel in the cup → double shot → 6 oz milk at 140°F → a thread of
caramel and flaky salt on top.

**The move:** take the caramel one shade darker than feels safe. Pale caramel is just
sweet; properly dark caramel is bitter enough to stand up to both the espresso and the
miso. It should smell faintly of smoke right before you kill it with the cream.
