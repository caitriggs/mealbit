Four ingredients, ninety-five cents, and stronger than anything a café will sell you. A
$8 aluminium *phin* filter is the traditional way and worth owning, but any strong brew
works.

**Build:** 2 tbsp sweetened condensed milk in the bottom of a glass → 2–3 oz very strong
hot coffee poured directly onto it → **stir until completely combined while hot** →
pour over a full glass of ice.

**The move:** stir it while it's hot, before the ice. Condensed milk is dense enough to
sit in a stubborn layer at the bottom of a cold glass forever; over ice you will be
scraping at it with a straw. Ten seconds of stirring while hot and it emulsifies
completely.

**Beans:** this is the one drink that wants a **dark, oily, chicory-heavy roast** — Café
du Monde or a Vietnamese brand. Your good light-roast single-origin will taste thin and
sour against that much condensed milk.
