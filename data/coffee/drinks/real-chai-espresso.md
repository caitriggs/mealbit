The café "dirty chai" is espresso poured into a syrup concentrate from a carton. Real
masala chai is boiled — tea, milk, water and whole spices all in one pot — and it costs
about forty cents a cup.

**Concentrate (makes ~6, keeps 5 days):** crush **8 cardamom pods, 6 cloves, 10 black
peppercorns, 1 tsp fennel seed** and a 2-inch piece of ginger in a mortar or under a pan.
Into a saucepan with 2 cups water, a cinnamon stick and ¼ cup sugar. **Boil hard 5
minutes** — not steep, boil; whole spices need it. Then 3 tbsp strong black tea, 2 minutes
more. Strain.

**Build:** 4 oz concentrate + 4 oz milk brought just to a simmer together, then a single
shot of espresso poured in.

**The move:** boil the spices in **water first, before any milk goes in.** Milk proteins
bind the aromatic compounds and blunt the extraction — this is why chai made by simmering
everything in milk from the start tastes flat and vaguely sweet instead of sharp and
peppery. Water first, milk second, tea last.
