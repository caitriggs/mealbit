The *shakerato* is Italian: espresso shaken hard with ice until it foams. It's the best
iced coffee technique there is and it takes forty seconds.

**Syrup (makes ~12):** ¾ cup brown sugar, ¾ cup water, 1 cinnamon stick, warmed until
dissolved. Off heat, 1 tsp vanilla and a pinch of salt.

**Build:** double shot + 1½ tbsp syrup into a cocktail shaker or a jar with a tight lid,
**with 4–5 ice cubes**. Shake **hard for 20 seconds** — you want to hear the ice breaking
down. Strain into a glass over fresh ice. Float 3 oz cold barista oat milk over the back
of a spoon so it layers.

**The move:** shake it while the espresso is still hot. Hot coffee hitting cold ice
emulsifies the coffee oils into a genuine crema-like foam — shake it cold and you just get
cold coffee. And **strain out the shaking ice**; it's watery and it'll dilute the glass.
