Sesame and coffee are both roasted seeds, which is exactly why this works. It's halva in
a cup, and it's the drink that converts people who think they don't like flavored coffee.

**Blend (makes ~8):** ¼ cup tahini, ¼ cup maple syrup, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp vanilla, a
big pinch of salt, whisked with **3 tbsp hot water** until it goes from seized-and-pasty
to smooth and pourable. (It *will* seize first. Keep whisking; more water fixes it.)

**Build:** 1½ tbsp paste in the cup → **double shot poured on and whisked in immediately**
→ 6 oz milk at 140°F. Flaky salt and a few toasted sesame seeds.

**The move:** whisk the paste into the hot espresso *before* the milk goes in. Tahini
added to a full cup of milky coffee never disperses and you get an oily slick on top and
a plug at the bottom. Hot espresso is concentrated and hot enough to emulsify it properly.
