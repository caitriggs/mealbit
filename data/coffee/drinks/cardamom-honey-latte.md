Green cardamom and honey is the oldest coffee flavoring in the world — Bedouin *qahwa*
has been made this way for centuries — and it makes a latte taste expensive with almost
no effort.

**Syrup (makes ~10 drinks):** ½ cup honey, ½ cup water, **12 green cardamom pods
cracked open with the flat of a knife**. Warm until just steaming — **do not boil honey**,
it goes flat and caramel-ish and loses its floral top notes. Off the heat, steep 30
minutes, strain.

**Build:** 1 tbsp syrup in the cup → double shot poured over → 6 oz milk at 140°F.
A **very** light dust of ground cardamom on the foam, plus a pinch of flaky salt.

**The move:** crack the pods, don't grind them. Pre-ground cardamom is dusty and slightly
bitter; the whole seeds inside a cracked pod give you the eucalyptus-citrus top note that
makes people ask what it is.
