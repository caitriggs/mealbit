A proper mocha is made with **melted chocolate, not chocolate syrup**, and that single
change is why café mochas taste like a drink and home ones taste like hot cocoa with
coffee in it.

**Ganache (makes ~8, keeps 2 weeks):** 4 oz 70% dark chocolate chopped, 4 oz hot cream
poured over, left 2 minutes, then stirred from the center outward until glossy. Stir in
**the zest of a whole orange**, ¼ tsp ground cardamom and a big pinch of salt.

**Build:** 2 tbsp ganache in the cup → double shot → stir to melt completely → 6 oz milk
at 140°F. A wide strip of orange peel twisted over the top, then dropped in.

**The move:** zest, not juice. Orange juice curdles milk and reads as sour; the zest
carries the oils, which is where the actual orange smell lives. Zest it directly over the
warm ganache so the oils land in the fat.
