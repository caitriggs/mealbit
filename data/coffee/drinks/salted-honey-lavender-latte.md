Lavender is the most over-done flavor in coffee and it's over-done because people use too
much. Get the dose right and it's genuinely lovely; get it wrong and you're drinking
potpourri.

**Syrup (makes ~10):** ½ cup honey, ½ cup water, **2 teaspoons culinary lavender — not a
tablespoon, not "a few sprigs."** Warm until just steaming, off the heat, **steep exactly
15 minutes**, then strain immediately. Stir in ¼ tsp fine salt.

**Build:** 1 tbsp syrup → double shot → 6 oz milk at 140°F (or over ice with cold milk).
Flaky salt on top.

**The move:** the timer. Lavender extracts fast and then keeps extracting into soapy
bitterness — at 40 minutes it is ruined and unfixable. Set an actual timer, strain at 15,
and the salt at the end pulls the whole thing back from perfume toward food.

**Buy culinary lavender**, not craft-store lavender, which is often sprayed.
