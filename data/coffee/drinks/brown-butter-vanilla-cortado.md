A cortado is a double shot cut with an equal volume of milk — about 4 oz total. It's the
drink for people who find a 12 oz latte to be mostly milk, and the small volume means
every flavor has to be right.

**Brown butter syrup (makes ~10):** 4 tbsp butter melted over medium until the milk solids
at the bottom turn deep gold and it smells of toffee — **watch it, the window between
brown and burnt is about twenty seconds.** Off the heat, whisk in ½ cup brown sugar and
¼ cup hot water, then 1 tsp vanilla and a big pinch of salt. **Keep it refrigerated and
warm it before use** — it solidifies.

**Build:** 2 tsp syrup in a small glass → double shot → **only 2 oz milk** at 140°F,
textured thin and glossy, not foamy.

**The move:** at cortado volume, milk texture matters more than in any other drink. You
want *microfoam* — wet, paint-like, barely any bubble — because there's no volume to hide
behind. This is the drink to practice your pour on.
