You make the *milk*, not a syrup. Rice and cinnamon soaked overnight and blended is
horchata, it costs about thirty cents a quart, and over espresso it's remarkable.

**Horchata (makes ~6, keeps 4 days):** 1 cup long-grain rice + 2 cinnamon sticks + 4 cups
water, **soaked overnight** on the counter. Blend the whole thing — rice, sticks, water —
for a full 2 minutes. **Strain twice through a fine sieve** (or once through a nut-milk
bag). Sweeten with ⅓ cup sugar and 1 tsp vanilla.

**Build:** glass of ice → 6 oz horchata → 1 tbsp sweetened condensed milk stirred in →
double shot poured over the top so it marbles. Grated cinnamon.

**The move:** the overnight soak is not optional and neither is the double strain. Under-
soaked rice blends into grit that settles at the bottom of the glass; a single strain
leaves it chalky. Get it right and it's silky enough that people assume it's dairy.
