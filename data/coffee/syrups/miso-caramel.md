The strangest thing in the box and probably the best. White miso is fermented, salty and
faintly sweet — it does what salt does to caramel, with four times more going on. It reads
as "what *is* that", not "this has miso in it".

¾ cup sugar melted dry over medium until it's the colour of an old penny — **swirl, don't
stir.** Off the heat, whisk in ½ cup warm cream (it will erupt; normal), then 2 tbsp
butter, **1½ tbsp white miso**, 1 tsp vanilla.

**The move:** take the caramel one shade darker than feels safe. Pale caramel is only
sweet; properly dark caramel is bitter enough to stand up to both espresso and miso. It
should smell faintly of smoke right before the cream goes in.
