Savoury, piney and completely non-obvious over cold espresso. The 3pm drink that isn't a
fourth cup of coffee.

½ cup honey, ½ cup water, **4 rosemary sprigs**. Warm to dissolve, off the heat, steep
**20 minutes and no longer**, strain.

**The move:** the timer. Past 20 minutes rosemary goes medicinal and there's no rescuing
it. Same rule as lavender, same reason.
