The one to always have. It's the base note under half the café menu, and it makes a plain
latte taste finished.

1 cup sugar, 1 cup water, 2 cinnamon sticks, a split vanilla bean (seeds scraped in, pod
too). Warm until the sugar dissolves — **do not boil**. Off the heat, steep 30 minutes,
strain, add a pinch of salt.

**The move:** the salt. A quarter teaspoon in a cup of syrup makes it read as *rounder*
rather than sweeter, and it's why café syrups taste different from sugar water.
