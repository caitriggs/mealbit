Lavender is the most over-done flavour in coffee, and it's over-done because people use
too much of it.

½ cup honey, ½ cup water, **2 teaspoons culinary lavender — not a tablespoon.** Warm to
just steaming, off the heat, **steep exactly 15 minutes**, strain immediately. Stir in
¼ tsp fine salt.

**The move:** set an actual timer. Lavender extracts fast and then keeps going into soapy
bitterness; at 40 minutes it's unfixable. The salt at the end pulls it back from perfume
toward food. Buy **culinary** lavender — craft-store lavender is often sprayed.
