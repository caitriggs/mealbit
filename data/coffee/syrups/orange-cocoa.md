A real mocha note without a tub of chocolate sauce.

¾ cup sugar, ¾ cup water, ⅓ cup cocoa powder whisked in off the boil until no lumps
remain, then **the zest of two whole oranges**, 1 tsp vanilla, a big pinch of salt.
Simmer 3 minutes, strain.

**The move:** zest, never juice. Juice curdles milk and reads sour; the zest carries the
oils, which is where the smell of orange actually lives. Zest it straight into the warm
syrup so the oils land in something.
