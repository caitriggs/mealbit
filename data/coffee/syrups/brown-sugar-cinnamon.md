Molasses gives it a depth white sugar can't reach. This is the syrup under every "brown
sugar oat shaken espresso" on a menu board.

¾ cup dark brown sugar, ¾ cup water, 1 cinnamon stick. Warm to dissolve, off the heat,
1 tsp vanilla and a big pinch of salt.

**The move:** dark brown, not light. Light brown sugar is barely distinguishable from
white here; dark carries enough molasses to survive being poured over ice and milk.
