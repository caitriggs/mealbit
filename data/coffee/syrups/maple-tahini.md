Sesame and coffee are both roasted seeds, which is exactly why it works. Halva in a cup.

¼ cup tahini, ¼ cup maple syrup, ½ tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp vanilla, a big pinch of salt,
whisked with **3 tbsp hot water** until it stops being a seized paste and turns pourable.
It *will* seize first — keep going, more water fixes it.

**The move:** whisk it into the hot espresso *before* the milk. Added to a full milky cup
it never disperses and you get an oily film on top and a plug at the bottom.
