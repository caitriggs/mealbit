The oldest coffee flavouring there is — Bedouin *qahwa* has been made with cardamom for
centuries.

½ cup honey, ½ cup water, **12 green cardamom pods cracked under a knife**. Warm until
just steaming — **never boil honey**, it flattens and loses the floral top. Steep 30
minutes, strain.

**The move:** crack whole pods, don't use ground. Ground cardamom is dusty and slightly
bitter; the seeds inside a cracked pod give the eucalyptus-citrus note people can't place.
