The prettiest thing you can put on a latte — bright green against pale foam.

¾ cup shelled pistachios pulsed to a coarse rubble (**not a powder** — stop early), 2 tbsp
sugar, 1 tsp crushed dried rose petals, ¼ tsp cardamom, flaky salt.

**The move:** pulse, don't blend. Three or four one-second pulses. Pistachios go from
rubble to oily paste in about ten seconds and there is no way back.
