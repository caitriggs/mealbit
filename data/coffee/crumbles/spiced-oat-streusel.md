Tastes like the top of a coffee cake, which is exactly the point.

½ cup rolled oats, ⅓ cup flour, ⅓ cup brown sugar, 1 tsp cinnamon, ½ tsp cardamom,
¼ tsp nutmeg, ½ tsp salt, rubbed together with 4 tbsp cold butter until it clumps. Bake
at 350°F for 15 minutes, stirring once. Cool completely before jarring.

**The move:** cool it *completely*, on the tray, before it goes anywhere near a lid.
Warm streusel steams itself soft in a jar and you lose the whole point of it.
