The laziest thing in the box and the one that most reliably makes a drink look like it
came from somewhere expensive. No cooking at all.

½ cup cacao nibs, ½ cup demerara sugar, a pinch of cinnamon, a pinch of flaky salt.
Shake in a jar.

**The move:** nibs, not chocolate chips. Nibs are unsweetened and bitter, so they cut the
sugar in the drink instead of adding to it, and they stay crunchy floating on foam.
