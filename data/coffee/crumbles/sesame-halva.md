Crumbled halva melts slightly on hot foam and goes fudgy. It is very good and almost
nobody has had it on a coffee.

Toast ¼ cup sesame seeds in a dry pan until they pop and smell nutty, 3 minutes. Cool.
Crumble 3 oz halva by hand into pea-sized pieces, toss with the seeds and flaky salt.

**The move:** crumble by hand, and keep it dry. Any moisture and the halva clumps into
one solid brick in the jar.
