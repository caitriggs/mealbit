Brown butter and coffee is one of the great pairings and almost nobody does it at home.

Brown 4 tbsp butter until the solids go deep gold and it smells of toffee — **the window
between brown and burnt is about twenty seconds.** Rub into ¾ cup flour, ¼ cup sugar,
½ tsp salt and 1 tsp vanilla until it clumps. Bake at 325°F for 15 minutes. Cool, crush
coarse.

**The move:** watch the pan, not the clock. Take it off when it smells like toffee, and
pour it straight out of the hot pan or it keeps cooking and turns bitter.
