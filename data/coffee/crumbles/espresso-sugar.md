Made from the spent puck. Costs nothing, takes three minutes, and is the most
coffee-tasting thing you can put on a coffee.

Dry a few spent espresso pucks — break them up on a tray, 200°F oven for 30 minutes, or
just leave them out overnight. Blitz with ½ cup sugar and a pinch of flaky salt until it
looks like dark sand.

**The move:** the puck has to be genuinely bone dry or the sugar cakes. This is also the
only crumble that gets better the more coffee you drink.
