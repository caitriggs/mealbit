The one worth the extra fifteen minutes, and it makes the kitchen smell extraordinary.

Peel three oranges in wide strips, **scrape the white pith off the back** — pith is the
bitterness. Simmer the strips in ½ cup sugar and ½ cup water for 15 minutes until
translucent. Drain, dry on a rack, chop fine. Toss with 1 tsp ground cardamom, 1 tbsp
sugar and flaky salt.

**The move:** the pith. Skipping that scrape is the difference between candied peel and
something acrid you'll throw away.
