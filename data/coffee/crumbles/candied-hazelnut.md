---
title: Candied hazelnut crumble
kind: crumble
makes: 20 drinks
keeps_weeks: 8
storage: airtight jar, room temperature
active_time: 15
ingredients:
  - 1 bag raw hazelnuts [pantry]
pantry: [granulated sugar, flaky salt, butter]
tags: [nutty, crunchy, workhorse]
pairs_with: [vanilla-cinnamon, miso-caramel, orange-cocoa]
---

Toast 1 cup hazelnuts at 350°F for 10 minutes, then **roll them in a tea towel while hot**
to slip the skins off. Melt ⅓ cup sugar dry until amber, stir in the nuts and 1 tbsp
butter, tip onto parchment, cool hard, then smash into rubble with the bottom of a pan.
Flaky salt through it.

**The move:** the tea towel. Hazelnut skins are bitter and they will not come off cold.
